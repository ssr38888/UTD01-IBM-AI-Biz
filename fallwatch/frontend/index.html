How intensity affects the estimate

Heuristic motion bands: high requires at least 1 body height/sec; medium requires at least 0.4 body heights/sec; slower measurable falls are low. At least three visible poses covering half the recorded observations, with visible standing and floor endpoints, are required. Gaps may not exceed 500 ms or 75% of the descent. Partial coverage is labeled Estimated / limited visibility. These image-plane measures do not establish physical impact force. Assumed relative dispute-rate reductions: high 25–35%, medium 10–25%, low 0–10%. Verified photos without measurable intensity use a 0–10% photo-only assumed reduction range. These adjustments are not learned from dispute outcomes.

Pipeline

The camera keeps watching. The server stores confirmed falls.

01 / Detect

On-device pose analysis checks steady standing, horizontal descent within two seconds, a hip drop of at least 0.15 body heights, and 300 ms confirmation.

02 / Preserve

JPEG photos and the pose log are saved with a manifest and chained SHA-256 fingerprints. Integrity is verified by the server.

03 / Assess

Measured speed gives high, medium, or low motion intensity; verified photos can also be assessed without a measured descent. Assumed relative reductions are 25–35%, 10–25%, and 0–10%; the population baseline is not an employee-specific prediction.
